Find cameras on your network

  
Looks for anything that serves RTSP video, plus cameras that announce themselves (ONVIF). Takes about 15–30 seconds.

  

    

    

    
Most cameras need their login before they'll share video. Only used for this test, never saved. If you have several cameras with different logins, scan once per login.
